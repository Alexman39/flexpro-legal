# FlexPro privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

FlexPro is a strength-training app. This policy explains what data it handles, why, where it is
kept and what you can do about it. We keep it short and specific: if something isn't listed
here, FlexPro doesn't collect it.

## Who is responsible

Alexandros Maniateas, Kaisariani, Athens, Greece — the "controller" under the GDPR.
Contact: maniateas52@gmail.com

## What FlexPro stores, and why

| Data | Why | Legal basis (GDPR) |
|---|---|---|
| **Your account:** email address, name and account id from Google or from your email sign-up | To sign you in and keep your data separate from other people's | Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| **Your training data:** workouts, sets (weight, reps, reps in reserve), notes, exercise swaps, and your answers to the onboarding questions (experience, goal, days per week, session length, equipment, language) | To run the app: log workouts, show your history and records, recommend a program; and to back them up, so they come back on a new phone | Your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a)), because training data can count as health data |
| **Your consent choices** and when you made them | To prove and respect what you agreed to | Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) |
| **Crash reports:** what went wrong, the device model, the Android version, the app version | To find and fix crashes, especially during workouts | Legitimate interest in a reliable app (Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| **Usage analytics** (screens opened, features used, without your training data) — **only if you turn it on** | To learn which features help people | Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)); off by default |

FlexPro does **not** sell your data, show ads, track you across other apps, or use your data to
train AI models.

## Where your data is kept

- **Training data is kept on your phone and backed up in the EU.** The backup uses Cloud
  Firestore by Google, stored in its `eur3` multi-region (Belgium and the Netherlands), so your
  workouts come back if you lose or change your phone. Only your signed-in account can read or
  change your backup.
  A workout you are still training stays on the phone until you finish it.
- **Sign-in** uses Firebase Authentication by Google. Your email, name and account id are kept by
  Google, which may process them outside the EU under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and
  standard contractual clauses.
- **Crash reports and optional analytics** use Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for
  Firebase, under the same terms.

Google's terms: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## How long we keep it

- Training data and onboarding answers, on the phone and in the backup: until you delete them or
  your account. When you delete a workout, the backup keeps only a marker (its id and when it was
  deleted) so your other phones delete it too; the marker goes when you delete your account.
- Account data: until you delete your account.
- Crash reports: up to 90 days. Analytics data: up to 2 months (the shortest Google offers).

## Your rights

You can, at any time:
- **Export** all your training data as a CSV file (Profile or History → Export CSV).
- **Delete your account and all data** in the app (Profile → Delete account and data), backup
  included.
- **Withdraw consent:** turn analytics off in Profile; withdrawing consent for training data means
  deleting your data, because the app can't work without it.
- Ask us for access to, correction of, or a copy of your data, or object to processing:
  maniateas52@gmail.com.
- Complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (www.dpa.gr) or the authority where you live.

## Age

FlexPro is for people aged 16 or older.

## Changes

If we change what we collect or why, we update this page and the date above, and ask for your
consent again when the law requires it.
